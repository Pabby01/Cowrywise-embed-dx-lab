KoloVault — Disciplined Savings & Target Goals Web App

A full-stack savings application allowing consumer fintech users to create target milestone funds and disciplined locked vaults with guaranteed annual returns (+12.5% to +15.5% APY). Includes countdown maturity timers, 24-hour cooling-off withdrawal locks, and automated interest calculators.

SpareStash — Checkout Spare Change Round-Up Engine

Embed automated micro-investing into shopping checkout carts or debit card spend feeds. Automatically rounds up checkout balances to the nearest ₦100 or ₦500 and triggers an automatic sweep into high-yield mutual funds once accumulated spare change reaches ₦1,000.

Cowrywise Embed Endpoints Used
POST /api/v1/wallets/transfer POST /api/v1/assets/invest POST /webhooks (wallet.funded) GET /api/v1/accounts

CorpYield — Automated Payroll Wealth Split & Treasury Dashboard

A corporate employee benefit system enabling HR platforms to empower corporate workers to automatically split net salaries into disciplined savings and sovereign Eurobonds before direct bank disbursement ("Pay Yourself First"). Includes automated virtual account generation and webhook listeners.
