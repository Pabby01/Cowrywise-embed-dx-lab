Scenario 1: Neobank & Consumer Fintech SuperApp

Context & Business Goal

A digital banking app with 500,000 active retail accounts wants to increase user retention and deposit stickiness by offering automated high-yield savings and mutual fund investments directly from their primary checking balance.

Architecture & Data Flow

  1. Unified KYC Propagation: Since the neobank has already performed BVN verification during customer onboarding, their backend automatically provisions a Cowrywise account via api.accounts.createAccount() and verifies identity via api.accounts.updateIdentity() in a background worker, removing redundant customer friction.
  2. Automated "Round-Up" Savings:
    • Every time a customer spends using their debit card (e.g., spending ₦1,420 at a supermarket), the system rounds up to the nearest ₦100 (₦80 spare change difference).
    • Once accumulated spare change hits ₦1,000, the app funds the user's investment wallet and triggers an automated buy order into the Cowrywise Money Market Fund.
  3. Consolidated In-App Net Worth Card: The mobile app queries getPortfolioSummary() to render a consolidated net-worth breakdown directly beside the user's active checking account balance.
Key Benefit: Customers keep higher balances within the neobank ecosystem rather than transferring funds out to competing wealth management apps.

Scenario 2: HR Tech / Payroll & Remuneration Platform

Context & Business Goal

A modern B2B SaaS payroll provider wants to empower corporate employees to automatically split their monthly net salaries into disciplined wealth products before the funds reach their retail bank accounts ("Pay Yourself First").

Architecture & Data Flow

  1. Employee Opt-In & Allocation Slider: In the employee self-service portal, employees configure a monthly wealth allocation (e.g. 10% to Emergency Savings, 15% to Sovereign Eurobonds, 75% to liquid checking).
  2. Direct Payroll Split via Virtual Accounts:
    • On payday, the employer disburses the designated 25% directly to Cowrywise Embed wallets via dedicated virtual accounts.
    • The payroll platform listens for the wallet.funded webhook event.
    • Upon receiving the webhook, the platform automatically calls api.savings.createSavingsPlan() and api.assets.investInAsset() on behalf of each participating employee.
  3. Tax & Regulatory Reporting: Employees download a certified annual statement showing interest earnings and capital gains for statutory tax returns.

Scenario 3: E-Commerce & Retail Loyalty Rewards ("Wealthback")

Context & Business Goal

A high-volume retail marketplace replaces traditional points-based loyalty cards with "Wealthback" — converting merchant cashbacks and reward points into fractional government Treasury Bills and mutual fund units.

Architecture & Data Flow

  1. Fractional Wealthback Rewards: When a customer completes a purchase, the merchant issues 2% cashback. Rather than expiring store credit, the reward balance accumulates in an escrow wallet.
  2. Automatic Threshold Investment: When rewards reach ₦1,000 for money market funds or ₦5,000 for equity index funds, the platform sweeps the funds into the customer's Cowrywise account.
  3. Gamification & Growth Loop: Users open the marketplace weekly to observe their cashback growing through compound interest, drastically increasing monthly active users (MAU).
